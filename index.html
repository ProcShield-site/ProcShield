WHY WE EXIST

Vision & mission

Vision

At PROC SHIELD Private Limited, we envision a bold and transformative future where our company stands as the vanguard in shaping the next generation of secure hardware. From pioneering embedded systems to crafting state-of-the-art System-on-Chips (SoCs) and high-end, domain-specific architectures, we are on a mission to redefine the very fabric of secure computing. Our vision transcends conventional boundaries as we strive to be at the forefront, setting new industry benchmarks for secure hardware solutions.

Mission

In an era dominated by digital transformation, PROC SHIELD recognizes the growing concerns surrounding the security and privacy of user data, and the rise of stringent data protection regulations such as the GDPR. This concern also extends to outsourced chip manufacturing, where untrusted partners contribute to IC assembly — underscoring the need to secure the IC supply chain using obfuscation. Our mission is to safeguard user privacy and to defend against a growing range of sophisticated hardware attacks and vulnerabilities.
