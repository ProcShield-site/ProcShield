WHY WE EXIST

Vision & mission

Vision

To lead the next generation of secure hardware — from embedded systems to custom System-on-Chips and domain-specific architectures — and to set the industry benchmark for what secure computing should look like in an increasingly connected world.

Mission

To protect user privacy as data regulation tightens worldwide, and to defend the IC supply chain from untrusted manufacturing partners through hardware obfuscation — delivering secure-by-design hardware for general-purpose and specialized computing alike.
